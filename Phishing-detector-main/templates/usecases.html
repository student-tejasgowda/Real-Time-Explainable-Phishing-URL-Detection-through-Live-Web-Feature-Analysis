Use case Scenarios of Phishing attacks (Data Breach examples)

  • Use Case Scenario #1

    John Podesta’s Email (2016)
    Attackers pretending to be Google sent an email telling Podesta to change his password after an alleged hack. The malicious link led to account takeover and thousands of emails were later leaked via WikiLeaks.

  • Use Case Scenario #2

    BenefitMall (2018)
    Employee email credentials were exposed in a phishing attack, impacting customer data such as names, emails, DOBs and bank information, affecting a large network of businesses.

  • Use Case Scenario #3

    Sony Pictures (2014)
    Attackers used phishing and spear-phishing with malware to breach Sony’s network, exfiltrate confidential data and disable systems — costing the company heavily.

  • Use Case Scenario #4

    Methodist Hospitals – Gary, Indiana
    Phishing compromised staff email accounts exposing patient records (names, SSNs, insurance, medical data).

  • Use Case Scenario #5

    University vendor fraud (2019)
    A phishing-initiated change to vendor routing numbers cost a university $315,000 in fraudulent transfers.
